Why use this tool?

Passport Seva rejects photos that do not meet exact specifications. Many applicants — especially NRIs and students abroad — waste hours resizing, compressing, and re-uploading before getting it right. This converter handles face detection, cropping, background whitening, and compression automatically.

Passport Seva photo requirements

  • Dimensions: 630 × 810 pixels
  • Format: JPEG, file size under 250 KB
  • Background: plain white
  • Face centered, looking straight at the camera
  • No glasses, no shadows on face or background
  • Head and shoulders visible

How it works

  1. Upload a JPG or PNG portrait
  2. The app detects your face and crops to passport proportions
  3. Background is whitened and the image is compressed to under 250 KB
  4. Preview, adjust if needed, and download your passport-ready JPEG
